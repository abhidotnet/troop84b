About Troop 84

A Scouts BSA (Boy Scouts) unit located in North Wales, PA.

Our troop

Troop 84 is a Scouts BSA unit in North Wales, PA. Scouts plan and lead their own activities, from weekly meetings to campouts, while earning ranks and merit badges along the way.

Boy Scouts starts at age 11, after Cub Scouts/Webelos, and goes until age 18.
